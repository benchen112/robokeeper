Findings

  • Tracking and 3D results match the previous pipeline: crossings agree within 5 cm; the 7 m first-0.2 s check is worse (25 vs 8 cm), the later checks slightly better.
  • Depth is noisy at range (7 m points scatter ±0.3 m in depth) because the calibration is provisional and disparity is small; a checkerboard calibration is the next accuracy step.
  • Laptop replay, old → new, run back to back: median pair time 801→235 ms (2 m), 168→55 ms (4 m), 130→49 ms (7 m); p95 2–7× lower. Pi timing not yet measured.
  • The close-up ball (≲2 m) is still the slowest case, and the 11 m clip is not shown: both pipelines track letters on a passing shirt there, not the ball.
