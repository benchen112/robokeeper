Findings

  • The rolling ball is tracked in both eyes from kick to frame exit, from ~22 px radius at 7 m up to ~180 px beside the camera.
  • Disparity is consistent with the marked distances. Ball size and disparity both imply a focal length of roughly 1300 px; with the 30 cm baseline the starts come out near 6.7 m and 4.1 m. Uncalibrated estimate.
  • The eyes are vertically misaligned: the right eye sees the ball ~35 px lower than the left. Stereo calibration and rectification are needed before 3D triangulation (step 3).
  • One-eye false positives after the ball leaves (kicker's hand and shoe, 7 m frames 279–321). Requiring both eyes to agree would reject them.
  • The resting ball is not acquired until it moves (motion-triggered detector), and centers are less accurate when the ball is cut off by the frame edge.
  • Processing is 200–1200 ms per pair, fine offline but far from the 16.7 ms budget for live 60 fps (step 4).
